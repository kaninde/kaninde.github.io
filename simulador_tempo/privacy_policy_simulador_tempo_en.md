# Privacy Policy – SimulaTempo

Last updated: October 22, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how **SimulaTempo** (“App”) handles information on your device and, when you use certain features, limited interactions over the internet.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how data is handled.

---

## 2. Data Collection and Storage

### 2.1 Local data storage

- The App is designed so that **core content** — including **simulation settings** (duration, answer-sheet reserve, board interval, name/title), **exam sessions**, **history records**, **answer-sheet evaluations**, **saved training templates** (when available), **preferences** (theme, palette), **local report grants**, and related records — is stored **locally** on your device (for example in a local SQLite database `simulatempo.db`).
- You can **export** or **share** locally generated files (for example per-record or batch CSV when unlocked), **delete** records in History, or use wipe options in Settings when available.

### 2.2 Accounts

- The App **does not require** you to create an account for the main local workflow.

### 2.3 Data we do not operate as a training cloud backend

- SimulaTempo is **not** a cloud sync service for simulations and **does not** run a backend whose purpose is to store your full history on **our** servers as part of normal day-to-day use.
- When you use **share**, **export CSV**, or open a file, content is sent through **your** device’s operating system (for example to another app or a path you choose). That is under **your** control.

### 2.4 Network use (store, ads, verification, optional remote content)

When you choose features that need connectivity, the App may connect to the internet, for example to:

- **In-app purchases / subscriptions:** handled by **Google Play** or **Apple App Store** under their terms and privacy policies.
- **Receipt verification (when configured):** the App may send technical purchase data (for example product/transaction identifiers provided by the store) to an HTTPS **verification service** configured in the build — **not** simulation content (questions, exam notes, etc.).
- **Advertising (if enabled):** ad SDKs may process **device and usage signals** as described in their own policies. Rewarded ads are optional and start only on an explicit gesture.
- **Optional remote JSON or links:** for example a lightweight **“other apps”** carousel or similar configuration hosted on a **third-party** URL included in the build; such requests go to whoever operates that host. Opening **documentation**, **support**, or **store** links also uses the network.

### 2.5 Permissions

Depending on the features and platform, the App may request access to capabilities such as:

- **Files / storage (as required by the OS):** for **exporting** or **sharing** CSV and temporary files, using system pickers/share sheets where available.
- **Internet:** for **ads**, **billing**, receipt verification, optional remote configuration, or opening links.
- **Keep screen on / background execution (as applicable):** to preserve the timer session during training, in line with the permissions declared for your build.

The App does not access these resources without **your** action where the system requires explicit consent. Ads privacy preferences (UMP) may be managed in Settings when applicable.

---

## 3. Data usage

Data you provide is used to:

- Run **Configure**, **Exam**, **Record**, **History**, **reports**, **CSV export**, **saved templates**, and **Settings** screens;
- Apply **local grants**, **Plus/Pro**, and **rewarded ad** flows where implemented and enabled;
- Show **ads** or commercial surfaces when enabled in your build.

We do **not** use your simulation content (settings, times, evaluations) to build an advertising profile **on our side**; third-party ad networks may process data under **their** policies when ads are shown.

---

## 4. Data sharing

- We **do not sell** your personal information.
- Your **training history content** is not uploaded to **our** servers as part of the default local workflow described above.
- **App stores**, **ad networks**, the **receipt verification service** (when configured), and **hosts of optional remote URLs** you fetch may process data according to **their** policies when you use those services.

Any sharing of exported files (CSV) or screenshots happens only when **you** initiate share, export, or copy.

---

## 5. Third-party services

The App may rely on third parties, including:

- **Advertising:** may collect identifiers or usage data for ad delivery and measurement.
- **Payments:** processed by the platform store.
- **Purchase verification:** HTTPS host configured in the build when billing is active.
- **Hosts of optional configuration JSON:** whoever operates the URL you fetch may see **technical request data** (such as IP address) like any web server.

Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

---

## 6. Data retention and deletion

- Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
- You can use in-app options to **erase history records** or **remove local data** where provided.
- Uninstalling the App typically **permanently removes** local databases from the device.

You are responsible for securing your device (PIN, password, biometrics) and keeping the OS updated.
