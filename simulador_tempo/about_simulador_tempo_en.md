# About SimulaTempo

Welcome to **SimulaTempo** — an offline timer app for public exam candidates to train a full practice test: manage solving time, reserve time for the answer sheet, and review how they finished the simulation.

SimulaTempo organizes the flow **Configure → Exam → Record → History**. Set duration, answer-sheet reserve, and board interval; during the exam, follow remaining time, board milestones, and the answer-sheet alert; when you finish, log the result and browse history on the device. Training data stays **locally** on the device (SQLite). Reports, CSV export, and Plus/Pro features may be available depending on the build and store listing.

---

## Highlights

- **Configure:** optional name or auto title, exam duration, answer-sheet reserve, board interval, and start simulation.
- **Active exam:** central timer, progress, elapsed-time board milestones, visual answer-sheet alert, and controls (pause, recovery, fill answer sheet, finish).
- **Answer sheet:** dedicated step to mark when transcription starts without pausing the global timer; informational alert when the reserve begins.
- **Record:** close the simulation with answer-sheet evaluation and a finish summary.
- **History:** on-device records list, per-simulation detail, filters, and local pagination.
- **Per-record report and CSV:** local analysis of one simulation; on Free it may require a rewarded ad (when enabled); Plus/Pro unlock without an ad.
- **Saved templates (Pro):** store and reapply training configurations.
- **Period reports and batch CSV (Pro):** compare periods and export multiple records.
- **Settings:** Light/Dark/System theme, color palettes, Plus/Pro, ads privacy, About and help.
- **Privacy-first core:** history and training setup stay on the device unless you export/share a file or use features that need the internet (see the Privacy Policy).

Limits, prices, and benefits may change with updates; the **in-app** experience and store listing prevail over this page.

## FAQ

**Do I need an account?**  
No. You can use the core local workflow without creating an account.

**Do I need internet?**  
Day-to-day training (configure, exam, record, history) runs **offline**. Internet is used when you choose features that need it (for example **ads**, **in-app purchases**, receipt verification, loading optional **remote configuration** such as an “other apps” carousel, or opening external links).

**Will I lose my data if I uninstall?**  
Local data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app can **permanently remove** that data unless you have exported or shared copies yourself (for example CSV).

**Is it a question bank or auto-grading tool?**  
No. SimulaTempo is a **local timer and simulation log tool** — it does not grade answer sheets, compute board scores, or replace study materials.
