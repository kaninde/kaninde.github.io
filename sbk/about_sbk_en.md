# About Shelf & Box Keeper

Welcome to **Shelf & Box Keeper** — an offline app to catalog, organize, and find physical objects stored in boxes, drawers, rooms, and different properties, with data kept on your device.

Shelf & Box Keeper helps you map **locations**, **rooms**, **boxes**, and **items** in a simple hierarchy (Location → Room → Box → Item). Search globally in seconds, generate **QR codes** for boxes, attach **photos** and **tags**, track **loans**, and keep a light **movement history**. Your inventory stays **locally** on the device (SQLite). You can **export** and **import** a structured backup (ZIP/JSON) when you have premium access.

---

## Highlights

- **Home:** overview, shortcuts (locations, scanner, loans), recent items, tips.
- **Global search:** find items, boxes, tags, people, rooms, and locations offline — by name, tag, box code, or physical path.
- **Scanner:** read a box QR code and open its contents instantly.
- **Physical hierarchy:** locations (e.g. main home, beach house), rooms (garage, kitchen), boxes with friendly codes, and items inside.
- **Items:** name, quantity, description, notes, status, favorites, photos, and tags.
- **Loans:** register who borrowed what, due dates, and returns; dedicated loans tab.
- **Tags & favorites:** classify items and build quick lists.
- **Box QR codes:** generate, copy, print, or share unique codes per box.
- **Export / import:** local inventory backup when premium is unlocked.
- **Settings:** language, theme, subscription, about, and privacy links.
- **Privacy-first core:** your inventory stays on the device unless you export a file or use features that need the internet (see the Privacy Policy).

## Included features and limits (summary)

**Free tier (freemium):**

- Up to **1 location**, **1 room**, **2 boxes**, and **20 items** total (**cumulative** count — deleting does not free slots).
- Search, scanner, loans, tags, people, and other inventory flows without a daily usage cap while within limits.
- Banner ads in the main navigation.

**Premium** — unlock via **subscription**, **rewarded ad** (15 minutes of temporary premium), or **offline lifetime purchase**:

- Unlimited locations, rooms, boxes, and items.
- Export/import full backups and extra themes.
- No banner ads; “More apps” carousel hidden with premium.

Limits and entitlements can change with app updates; the **in-app** experience and store listing prevail over this page.

## FAQ

**Do I need an account?**  
No. You can use the core local workflow without creating an account.

**Do I need internet?**  
Day-to-day inventory work runs **offline**. Internet is used when you choose features that need it (for example **ads**, **in-app purchases**, loading optional **remote configuration** such as an “other apps” carousel, or opening external links).

**Will I lose my data if I uninstall?**  
Local data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app can **permanently remove** that data unless you have exported a backup (premium feature).

**Is it a commercial ERP or warehouse system?**  
No. Shelf & Box Keeper is a **local tool** for personal and household physical inventory — organizing boxes, objects, and loans — not advanced business stock management.
